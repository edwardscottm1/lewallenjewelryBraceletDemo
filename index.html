Build Your Own Bracelet

Design your own Ascent Bracelet online!

1. Choose a clasp by clicking on any clasp.
2. Select a bead and drag it onto the rope - starting with furthermost left and moving to the right.
3. Choose your rope by clicking on any rope.
4. Choose your wrist size. Measure your wrist with a tape measure (or string & ruler) and add 1.5 inches.
1. Choose a clasp by clicking on any clasp.
2. Choose your rope by clicking on any rope.
3. Click a bead to add it to the rope (click a bead on the rope to remove it).
4. Choose your wrist size. Measure your wrist with a tape measure (or string & ruler) and add 1.5 inches.
Hint: scroll the bracelet left and right to see the whole thing.

Cost will be added as you add the elements. And when you've gotten it just the way YOU want it, click to PURCHASE.
